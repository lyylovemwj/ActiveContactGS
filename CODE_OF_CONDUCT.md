# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment.

Be constructive, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and respect differences in background and experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the project or its contributors. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainer at <leiyuyi@mails.gdut.edu.cn>.
